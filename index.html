Who can buy from Alamo Peptide Labs?

Our products are sold to researchers and laboratory professionals aged 18 or older. By ordering, you confirm the materials will be used for laboratory research only.

What does “research use only” mean?

The products are intended for in-vitro and preclinical laboratory studies. They are not drugs, dietary supplements, or cosmetics, are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and must not be given to humans or animals.

Are your products approved by the FDA?

No. Our products have not been evaluated or approved by the FDA for any use.

Do you provide dosing or usage instructions?

No. We do not offer dosing, administration, or human-use guidance, and our team cannot answer questions of that kind. We can help with handling, storage, and documentation of the materials.

How do I get a Certificate of Analysis?

Each lot has its own COA. Contact support with the product name and the lot number printed on your vial to request it.

How is purity measured?

Purity is measured by HPLC and identity is confirmed by mass spectrometry. The percentage on a COA applies to the lot tested.

Where can I find shipping, payment, and return terms?

They are shown in the store policies and at checkout on alamopeptidelab.com, before you complete an order.
